design directions · sound-archive frontend

The galaxy.

The front door to the sound archive — the librarian over your own recordings. Two toggleable directions on one page (Cloud and Graph), both built on a true 3D projection of the vector space, running on synthesised data against the real serve API shapes so it's alive before klaxon is wired.

capture → catalog → surface → act / vouch → better surface

Pick a direction (top-left), then flip through the three views:

  • Galaxy — the hero. The whole archive as a 3D point-cloud from /api/points. Orbit, zoom, click a star to audition. Clusters carry colour.
  • Today — /api/surface?k=9: the morning pull, each card tagged with why it surfaced.
  • Search — /api/query filters + a text box (real text→audio lands with CLAP on klaxon).

Audio + waveforms are synthesised in your browser so playback actually works here — on klaxon these become the pre-rendered hooks (/hook), waveforms (/waveform) and full streams (/stream). Pick the one that feels right and it's just wiring from there.
